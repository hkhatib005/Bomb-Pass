Common questions

Do I need an account to play? No — every game works fully offline with no sign-in required. Signing in with Apple or email just lets you sync your Pro status across devices.

How do free rounds work? Bomb Pass and Chameleon each give you 2 free rounds per day. Every other game requires House Party Pro. Pro removes all limits.

How do I cancel a subscription? Manage or cancel anytime in your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. We don't have access to cancel it on your behalf.

How do I delete my account? Sign in, then go to Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and profile data.
